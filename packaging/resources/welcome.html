Oscilinea

Oscilinea is an audio visualizer plug-in for macOS. It passes your audio through unchanged and draws a focused shape display you can use in a DAW, record to a movie, mirror in a window, or publish as a Syphon source.

This installer adds Oscilinea in three forms:

  • Audio Unit — for Logic Pro, GarageBand, MainStage, and other AU hosts.
  • VST3 — for VST3 hosts.
  • Standalone app — runs on its own, no DAW needed.

This is a beta build for testing. To update later, just run the newer installer — it replaces this version in place and your existing projects keep working.
