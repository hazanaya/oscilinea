Installed

Oscilinea is ready to use:

  • In your DAW, rescan plug-ins (or just restart the DAW) and add Oscilinea to a track.
  • The standalone app is in your Applications folder. The first time you open it, macOS asks for microphone access — that is how it reads the audio you want to visualize.

The control panel starts hidden. Move the pointer near the bottom-center of the plug-in and click the handle to reveal the Signal, Look, and Record rows.

Found something? Send feedback to the person who shared this build. Updating later is just a matter of running the next installer.
